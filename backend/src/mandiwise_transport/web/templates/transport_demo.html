03 / Vehicle rates

What does a vehicle charge?

Cost for one vehicle = fixed charge + (rate per km × road distance). For a flat trip quote, enter it as the fixed charge and set the km rate to zero.

Keep 1–12 vehicle types. Give each a name, capacity and price. Starting rates are examples.

Uses the MandiWise Python transport engine.
Where do these numbers come from?
  1. Load: the farmer supplies the quantity.
  2. Distance: verify the road distance using a map or a known route.
  3. Capacity and rates: confirm them with the transport provider. Include agreed return travel and extra charges.

The project’s AGMARKNET/CEDA files contain crop prices and historical arrivals. They do not contain truck rates, road distances or available vehicles.
